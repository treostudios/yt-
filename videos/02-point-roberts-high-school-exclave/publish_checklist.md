## Paste-ready description

```text
Point Roberts and Blaine High School are both in Washington. But the road to school goes through British Columbia: two U.S.–Canada border crossings on the way there, and two more on the way home.

How did Washington end up with this little piece of land on the other side of the bay? Start with the Oregon Treaty of 1846.

The animation follows an example public road route, not a verified school-district itinerary. The dashed line measures shore to shore; it does not show a ferry route. Narration is synthetic.
```

### Timing and territory

Use **Studio → Analytics → Audience → When your viewers are on YouTube** if you have meaningful data. Without it, use **12:00 p.m. ET / 9:00 a.m. PT** as a starting slot: **10:00 p.m. Pakistan time during U.S. standard time**, or **9:00 p.m. during U.S. daylight time**. This weights the U.S. and Canada while still reaching the UK in its evening; it is not an Australia-first slot or a recommendation-system lever. Upload normally from your residential connection in Pakistan—no VPN, proxy, false location, irrelevant country tags or geography-wide distribution promises.

### What to check after launch

- **At 24 h:** Check playback, caption track and comments. Record Shorts-feed share, engaged views, Stayed to watch, AVD and APV. Keep the title stable unless correcting an actual error.
- **At 72 h and 7 days:** Compare #2 with #1 at the **same age**. Use the retention curve to separate the opening problem (0–3 s), treaty explanation (about 13–24 s), and ending (45–50 s). A high APV among people who stayed does not mean the hook held most feed impressions. Use engaged views instead of interpreting public view counts as unique viewers or qualified views.
- **Targets, not predictions:** ≥78% Stayed to watch, ≥120% APV (**1:00.42 AVD at 50.35 s**) and 97% combined **US + Canada + UK + Australia**. Note missing or withheld geography before interpreting the reported country share. If the share is low, develop the next episode around another place or contradiction those viewers are likely to care about; don’t try to change upload IP or add country-name tags. Don’t delete/repost a slow launch.
