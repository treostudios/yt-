## Description

Point Roberts and Blaine are both in Washington. But the road between them goes through British Columbia. Follow the high school commute: into Canada, back into the U.S., and twice more on the way home.

The Oregon Treaty of 1846 extended the border along the 49th parallel, leaving the tip of this peninsula on the U.S. side of the line.

Sources: Oregon Treaty text (Article I), Blaine School District, and the All Point Bulletin’s reporting on Point Roberts students.
