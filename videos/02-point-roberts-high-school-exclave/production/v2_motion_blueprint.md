# The Border Bus · V2 motion and audio blueprint

**55 seconds · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 1,650 frames.** This is the production specification. The [V2 master](../final_short_v2.mp4) implements its schematic-route mode with the rendering/data differences documented in the [production handoff](README.md#v2-master). The V1 MP4 stays intact. Use the locked voiceover below and the [frame cue sheet](v2_motion_cues.json). All quoted cues point to the existing voice take; pin to the actual waveform before locking a new read.

## 1. V1 teardown — what actually failed

| Evidence from the master or renderer | Why it feels junior | V2 correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `warpAffine` moves one baked terrain image at every scale. Relief, water, and roads have no independent depth. | Geographic scale changes, but viewer perspective does not. A zoomed image is not a camera passing over terrain. | One georegistered terrain rig with DEM geometry, draped vectors, continuous wide/medium/local levels of detail, and independently composited depth, shadows, and water. |
| The camera **does** interpolate; it is not a series of literal map cuts. But `ease_keys` re-eases every segment to zero velocity, especially at the 14.55–19.4 s wide hold. Labels enter by hard visibility thresholds, and chapter chips swap independently. | Each shot looks parked. The eye loses the origin/destination cue at each change, even though the background never hard-cuts. | Continuous position, target, and log-scale splines; one readable anchor is handed off per move. Use fade hysteresis and anchored leader lines, never a repositioned label without its pin. Remove the chapter chips. |
| “Same state” (4.06–6.0 s) happens while the framing favors Point Roberts; the distant school is not generally in the local frame. At 15.2 s the Blaine marker finally gets an isolated pulse. | The VO gives the contradiction before the frame proves it. The mid-video pulse does not repair the opening comprehension gap. | Bring both points into the same regional frame for the stressed words, and make the **same Washington fill** activate in both locations. Descend to the crossing while sharing the Point Roberts anchor. |
| Bus motion exists on the public Point Roberts approach, fades out by 8.48 s, and only returns at ~53.65 s. It always uses the same north-facing sprite. Later crossing sounds come from a timed counter and a separate panel rather than road/border intersections. | The named subject disappears for ~45 s. The panel is an honest disclosure, but it is not spatial evidence, and the icon never steers or participates in the homeward crossing. | Decouple honesty from passivity: verified road sections use distance-driven ground contact, steering, and intersection-triggered cues. Unverified sections use an active in-map geographic handoff, not a fake route or a disconnected box. |
| The white border is already visible; the treaty adds an east-to-west highlight between screen-space endpoints on a generalized latitude segment. There is no boundary-point traversal or changing map state tied to the cut-off tip. | The audience sees a title and a brighter line, not how the line created the anomaly. A latitude guide and a mapped boundary should not be treated as identical. | Split the IBC geometry into geographic segments; draw the relevant land segment by cumulative distance, reveal the peninsula fill immediately behind the head, and keep maritime segments out of the active reveal. |
| The count sits near the bottom; a U.S. flag card uses a fixed screen position, and the time graphic/late slip arrive as independent layers. | Attention has to jump map → UI → map. The gag takes over the scene instead of coming from it. | Animate reactions at the source: port badge → school pin → clock → paper slip hinged off the school pin. Only the last crossing gets a sum near the terrain; captions remain separate and fixed. |
| The closing shot brings back the opener’s title and a near-matching bus pose, not a deliberate match of camera and visual velocity. | It looks like a reset instead of a continuation to the next school morning. | Specify the loop as a camera and audio match. Resolve the fourth crossing first; then make an intentional next-day transition rather than teleporting or reversing a bus. |

**Directing rule:** Every factual clause gets one visible piece of evidence, at the word that names it. Make the map do the explaining: moving camera **or** moving vehicle **or** transforming feature takes the lead, not all three. Quiet holds on the joke and the landing are deliberate; they are not dead time. These changes improve the case for retention, not the ability to exclude any audience or predict completion.

## 2. Locked narration — no changes to the 55-second master

> This school bus leaves the country just to take kids to high school. And the school is in the same state.
>
> First stop: Canada. That’s the only road out of Point Roberts, Washington.
>
> That’s Blaine, right across Boundary Bay. It’s right there. Unfortunately, the bus is not a boat.
>
> Why is this tip even American? In 1846, the Oregon Treaty extended the border along the forty-ninth parallel. That left the tip on the U.S. side.
>
> So the bus goes through British Columbia, crosses back into the U.S. at Blaine, and pulls up to high school. That’s about forty minutes on the bus. One way.
>
> Then the last bell rings, and they do it all again. Into Canada. Back into Washington. Four border crossings, just to go to school in their own district.

`1846` is spoken “eighteen forty-six.” The late slip stays silent. No dialogue added by flag avatars, no customs procedures or passport stamps implied, and no schedule or alternate ending added. The compact captions retain the spoken wording; date and latitude may use numerals.

## 4. Geographic and animation implementation

### Data, QA, and route authorization for the edit

- **Boundary:** IBC NAD83 digital shapefile v1.3 as mapping reference; don't infer legal precision or replace it with `lat == 49.0`. Clip it to verified land polygons for the highlighted portion, leaving relevant maritime geometry dim or off. Describe the historical extension, not the entire modern western border as a straight line. Use [Article I](https://web.archive.org/web/20050113095649/http:/www.lexum.umontreal.ca/ca_us/en/cus.1846.28.en.html), [IBC digital boundary](https://www.internationalboundarycommission.org/en/maps-coordinates/coordinates.php) and [NASA geography context](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/point-roberts-148367/). Date the shoreline snapshot and check tidal-flat classification at both local scales.
- **Pinned anchors, not fake precision:** Boundary Bay–Point Roberts uses the real public Tyee Drive / 56 Street connection; compute the outbound and homebound triggers by intersecting each appropriate public road centerline with the IBC overlay. Approximate setup center `49.002° N, 123.068° W` is **only** a framing locator, not the trigger. Pin Blaine High School from its verified public campus at 1055 H Street (approximately `48.9927° N, 122.7382° W` for initial framing; register the actual footprint from the map extract). Use centroid/label points for Point Roberts and Blaine and a basin polygon for the bay. No private stops or student schedules. [3](https://www.pointrobertschamberofcommerce.com/getting-here/) · [Blaine High School](https://www.blainesd.org/o/blaine-high-school/) · [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaine_High_School_(Washington))
- **Full tracked mode is gated.** Reporting verifies the round trip through Canada, **not** the district’s current road-by-road corridor or east port. Get affirmative confirmation from district transportation or an authoritative publication before selecting ports and matching outbound/return road IDs. Peace Arch and Pacific Highway driving directions alone are not a school-bus assignment. Do not infer a campus bus entrance from the street address. Verify road connectivity, travel direction and correct grade separation independently for both legs. [5](https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/?date=20050613&slug=pointroberts13m) · [4](https://www.allpointbulletin.com/stories/parents-proposing-new-school-model,39739) · [3](https://www.pointrobertschamberofcommerce.com/getting-here/)
- **Default without confirmation:** Road-drape the vehicle only along the verified Point Roberts approach. Handoff to dynamic on-map country transitions and local rings/labels; no vehicle follows the unconfirmed corridor. Use `ROUTE SHOWN SCHEMATICALLY` during geographic summaries and no unconfirmed eastern pin. No physical vehicle hovering over water, no generic highway dressed up as the bus route, no guesses about inspection time. This protects the same cue timing in both modes. `≈40 MIN ONE WAY` is a reported commute estimate, not a speed calculation. [5](https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/?date=20050613&slug=pointroberts13m)

### Scene rig and styling

1. **Stage:** GEOlayers 3 geo-referenced map comps and AE shape layers for features; Helium or an external DEM renderer for real terrain/depth if available. Export one world transform for vectors, camera, labels, shadows and mesh. Use WGS84 geographic ingest, preserve source CRS, and reproject through a consistent origin into NAD83 / UTM zone 10N for the local solve (or an equivalent tangent-plane metric frame); document datum transforms. Use consistent vertically referenced, feathered land DEM patches and matching shore masks. Blend the overview LOD only after checking projected control-point overlap; if an anchor shifts, fix registration rather than crossfading the error. GEOlayers supports geographic features and animation; it does not verify a route. [1](https://aescripts.com/geolayers/)
2. **Camera:** `WORLD_ROOT → TRACK_NULL → AIM_NULL → CAMERA`. Author position, aim and log ground-width splines with shared tangents across cue nodes, not independent easy-ease segments. Stabilize geographic anchors in a mobile action band around x=130–900, y=350–1150 at export; blend targets before changing attention. Aim yaw within ±12° except for a deliberate local approach, and use ~5–22° pitch; put north back on screen for the historical beat. During the opener keep the same locator in the safe field; during the finish reach the same overview slightly early, settle through the same projected pose, and check last→first velocity as well as position. Dissolve from a bus to a **locator**, never an object changing world position across LOD.
3. **Grounded route:** Clean/merge actual road centerlines into ordered curves. Build a cumulative metric chainage `s`; sample `position(s)` over the terrain, `heading = tangent(s)`, and orient pitch from the smoothed local grade. Mount conforming wheel/contact shadows on the road/terrain, with a restrained, screen-size-clamped 3D bus body above them. Use a separate lane-agnostic render centerline, no unsupported twists or private entrances. Make the trim head follow the axle; as `s` first passes verified intersection chainage, the same event drives `line pulse → receiving fill → count → SFX`. Ease motion around actual turns; don't smooth geometry enough to cut corners. Fit arrival/traverse time by warping `s(t)`, not inventing geography or reverse-playing the inbound shot.
4. **Finish:** Deep navy `#081923`, teal water `#123741`, warm Washington `#D1AA73`, muted BC `#668873`, amber route `#FFD26E`, pale boundary `#ECE6D3`. Light from NW (~315°/40° as starting point). Real, low-amplitude delta relief (~1.1–1.3× locally), wide contact shadows, a narrow shoreline rim, and a dark seabed gradient. No fantasy cliffs at the border. Render separate diffuse/normal, ambient occlusion, depth, ID, vector and shadow passes where practical. Use constant screen-space cores (route 5–7 px, boundary 3–5 px with darker casing at 1080) plus short restrained glows; don't let glow jump water or mask lane/country contrast. Subtle occlusion does the depth work, not aggressive motion blur.
5. **Labels/cards/captions:** Use persistent projected anchors and short connected leaders. Point, label and shadows are distinct layers; world point stays fixed as camera moves. Auto-rank evidence and crossfade label state (town → campus) rather than pop at arbitrary scale. Cards use edge glide, eye-line to crossing/water, 4-frame blink, ≤1.4 s on-screen and no dialogue. Match the card flags and colors; don't lean on emojis, stereotypes, or hype. Captions 56–62 px semibold, 5 px dark outline and soft drop shadow, two lines max, center near x=500/y=1360, with projected graphic labels above. Adapt to actual Shorts app overlays before export.

## 5. Audio map, conform and release gates

- **VO:** One neutral North American baritone, intimate documentary read, dry through “not a boat” and “One way.” Print at 48 kHz; start voice on action, not on music. Leave 8–12 frames around the boat turn as room tone, not audition silence. Give “first stop” a controlled pocket, land “Canada” cleanly, and put documentary weight on “forty-ninth,” not on every word. The late slip has no speech. Preserve the master’s words if re-recording, then move the marker, lens acceleration, subtitle and hit **together** to the word, tolerating no more than ±2 frames on primary cues. A new VO audition/render is not included in this blueprint.
- **Stem logic:** Dialogue center; low coastal air/rolling tire bed; separate whooshes; separate geographic events and bell. Start VO high-pass 75–90 Hz, light de-essing and ~2–4 dB compression. Duck 3–6 dB under vocal consonants; avoid bass-only hits. Mechanical counts: lean 180–900 Hz with short tail, slight pitch distinction. Border draw: subtle tonal scrape under VO, settle in the gap. School: soft bell partial near return onset. Late slip: one quiet dry paper fold, not a laugh cue. Keep terrain reveals coherent in mute. Use ~−14 LUFS integrated / ≤−1 dBTP after encode as working targets; test phone, mono, and three concatenated loops. Carry bed across the edit with handles; no black frame or tail sting.
- **Conform and shipping gate:** Check checkpoints with leg-by-leg source evidence, compare road/border intersection pixels at 100% and mobile scale, and check every word anchor in sound-on and muted passes. Crossings must play `USA → CAN → USA → CAN → USA` once; no false mark during the first tease. Verify all title, city and country labels on actual overlay; validate terrain LOD as one camera solve with no sliding pins. At five seconds a cold viewer should identify both Washington endpoints and explain why Canada matters by the first crossing. At the final beat they should independently recount all four. If not, improve proof order rather than adding more rings. Log the reference input/version, measured caption timing, route verification state and credits in the V2 render manifest. This spec does not change the V1 MP4, timeline, or upload metadata.
